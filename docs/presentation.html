The Problem

Housing approvals are a maze.

🧩 Fragmented process

A single 10-unit project can require planning board review, building permits, fire plan review, sewer & water approvals, state environmental permits, and inspections — each with its own authority, forms, and sequence.

📄 Opaque requirements

Requirements live across municipal websites, PDFs, and state statutes (RIGL 45-23). Applicants discover missing documents only after rejection — losing weeks per review cycle.

⏱ No visibility

Once submitted, applicants can't see where their application stands, what's blocking it, or who needs to act next.

Rhode Island needs housing. Administrative friction shouldn't be the bottleneck.

The Solution

One workspace for the entire approval journey.

Register a 10-unit development in Cumberland and instantly see every applicable approval, the responsible authority, required documents, fee sources, and what to do next — all cited to official government sources.

Core Features

Built for how approvals actually work.

🧭 Pathway engine

Rule-based applicability: never assumes every approval applies. Unknown facts (e.g. sewer service) yield "conditional — confirm with DPW," not guesses.

✅ Smart checklists

Every item cites an official source with verification date. Fee items link to the fee schedule authority — amounts are never invented.

🔀 Workflow dashboard

Stages with explicit dependencies and parallel tracks. Completing a stage auto-activates the next. Nothing is "overdue" without a verified, cited deadline.

📁 Document management

Versioned uploads with SHA-256 integrity, auto-categorization, and per-checklist-item linking.

🏛 Review coordination

Record agency comments and RAIs (auto-creates tasks + notifications), draft responses, track decisions, conditions, and inspections.

🔐 Roles & audit

Six user roles with project-level access control, JWT auth, and a full audit log of every action.

AI Capabilities

AI that cites its sources.

💬 Approval assistant

Floating chat on every page with automatic project context. Answers are grounded via RAG over a curated database of official sources — every claim links to the authority, with verification dates.

🔍 Completeness checker

Two-pass analysis: deterministic rules (missing uploads, unit-count mismatches, missing signatures) + Claude reviewing extracted PDF text against the official checklist. Every finding must include verbatim evidence.

✍️ Draft responses

AI-drafted replies to agency review comments — always marked DRAFT and requiring professional review before submission.

No zoning analysis No invented fees or deadlines No approval predictions Uncertainty → "contact the authority" Works without an API key (rule-based fallback)

Trust by Design

Guardrails are the product.

📚 Verified source registry

Every procedural fact traces to an official source with authority, URL, version, and last-verified date. Staff updates require a change note — full change history kept.

🚫 Hard guarantees

  • Due dates rejected by the API unless a citing source is provided
  • Published processing estimates ≠ actual timelines, enforced in schema and UI
  • Decisions are only recorded as issued by authorities — never determined

⚖️ Honest completeness

Every automated check carries the disclaimer: only the responsible authority can determine official completeness. Findings ship with evidence, not vibes.
